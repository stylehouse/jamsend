⏸ RULED — the tap is parked and stays off. Two things the tap is not: it does not recompile or switch dialect (a runtime wrapper over the flat ghost bag, milliseconds to arm, disk untouched), and it is already per‑process. Line and branch tracing genuinely does wait on Atheory — it is a compiled Variant, the middle slot of TheA_<Name>_<Variant>_<dige>.
  1. Put the pictures in the room. BigWordland's searchbar hits are the Stemdex's regex defs; Atlas holds the compiler's real defs and calls for 585 docs, Electrode the measured flows. Neither reaches the room. First cut is not a new surface (the Lens prior‑art gate): feed the existing StemHive from Atlas rows and give the pin rail a → callers glyph.
  2. Rule the join. Atlas call,method:X,via:Y and Electrode Method:Y / Flow,of:X are the same edge, declared vs measured. Their diff is dead code, untested paths and hot paths in one query — and it is what cuts the TheA_ layers. Third L ghost, or a sub‑census inside Atlas?
  3. The Creduler_ensure gate. The loudest thing in every run and it is doing nothing: 720–1,152 version reads per run. A changed‑enrolment flag takes it to 36. It is the live spine, so your call.
  4. HELD — fold at the Story seam. Electrode_reduce is on demand; the natural automatic seam is the end of a step, where sworn evidence is already harvested — a per‑step picture for every Book. A few lines, parked with the rest of the tap.
  5. HELD — line and branch tracing needs the compiler and Atheory. A wrapper cannot see inside a body. The deep version is a compiled variant of a ghost, and its purpose is teaching the program's flow, not profiling it.
  6. Atheory — the manifest as particles. Three load lists still disagree (36 / 4 / 6). Electrode now gives the role dimension a measurement: arm on a humdinger, play a track, read which ghosts a music page actually touches.
