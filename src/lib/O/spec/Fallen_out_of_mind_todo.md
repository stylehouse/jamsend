## 2. THE ONE LAW, APPLIED FOUR TIMES AND FORGOTTEN IN BETWEEN

*A thing exists once under a container as its mainkey — the holding; anything naming it elsewhere is a
 referring particle wearing its own mainkey.* Born 2026-07-14 (`history/Radio_buildlog` — you read fresh
  snaps and named "fake-looking %Record cards that impersonate the audio holdings"), distilled into
   CLAUDE.md. Its sharpest phrasing is in `Springcore_meander`: *"A reference is its own word… the language
    never confuses being with pointing-at."* Its positive statement of what a husk IS: `Mag_todo §4.4` — *a
     husk is a rendering state of a Card* ("honesty about how much of a room you can actually reach is a
      feature, not a gap"), never a mainkey.

The husk bug is this law's fourth recurrence, and `Heist_todo §2` literally tables `%Record,husk:1,
 rummage:<seed>` as design. The invariant to install: **a `%Record` always has bytes or a `total` that
  promises them; identity-without-bytes is a `%Card`.** Two guards so it is not over-applied: a *worn*
   record (`Radio_todo §9.6`, "drop the buf, keep the husk") keeps `total` and stays legal; and the
    counter-rule `Mag_todo §11` — the identity law is about LIVE things; a ledger row must outlive its
     subject. Exactly two mint sites change: `Heist_census_heads` and `Heist_blag_folder`. The encoder move
      (brand `%Record` with neither `total` nor a chunk, the way it brands `undef`) follows `Repli_design
       §9.5`: presence-is-fill-state, `%Fill` is a tombstone, the promise is the Record's own `total`.

Related and unreconciled: `Cello_synthesis §R.8` — a `%Card` under a `%Library` may need a different prior
 than a `%Card` elsewhere: **the prior is keyed by context, not just mainkey**. The context *is* the referrer.
