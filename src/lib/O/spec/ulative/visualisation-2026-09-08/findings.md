## 0. The answer, in one paragraph

The glass never folded at the meaning because **the fold was never asked a question about
 meaning**.  Every collapse in the system — Voro's crush, Voro's gang, Vyto's fold scope, the
  proven-and-unwired ladder — is entered by a count (`members.length > budget`,
   `while (stats.visible > 15)`, `while (visible() > budget)`) and, once entered, partitions by a
    key chosen with the three meaning-bearing keys **explicitly removed**: `SIG_JOINS` strikes
     `id` and `of` as *"STRUCTURE, not meaning … plumbing"* (`vyto_foam.ts:19-21`), `Vyto_relate`
      strikes the mainkey too (`Vyto.g:1059`), and `bucket_key_of` skips every bare `1`
       (`vyto_foam.ts:58`) — which is every presence mainkey in the tree.  The mainkey survives
        only as the fold's *label* after the decision (`fold_kind` = the dominant child mainkey,
         `Voro.g:1515`) and as a *hue* (`Matstyle.svelte:376-383`).  So the glass has been folding
          at the crowd and colouring at the meaning.  Second, and this is the half the owner's
           complaint actually names: the round-trip law that would have caught the losses —
            *"`read(render(tree)) ≡ tree`, modulo declared dips"* (`Cstructures_todo.md:143`) — was
             built and gated at **one** hiding site (the distiller's crest, `Vyto_dip_assign`,
              VytoCrest's lying-door probe) and never extended to the other seven, which hide
               doorlessly and silently.  Two of those were **measured and named** on 2026-07-22
                with a *"HUMAN ruling owed (morning)"* (`Cstructures_todo.md:218-226`) and are
                 still open today.  They are literally the owner's *"randomly leave something off."*

## 2. What "meaning" is, in this codebase's own words

The corpus is not short of an answer.  It has one, it is precise, and it was **already applied to
 exactly this problem and then not built**.

**The metaphysics** (`CLAUDE.md`): a particle's type is its **mainkey** — *what the thing IS*.  A
 thing exists once under a shelf as its mainkey — the **holding**.  Anything that merely names it
  elsewhere is a **referring particle** wearing its own mainkey and carrying the id: `Card,id:X`
   beside `Record,id:X` for a 1:1, `Reco,by:X` / `Heist,of:X` for a many:1.  Three keys carry all
    of it: the mainkey, `of:`, and a shared `id`.

**The taxonomy** (`Cstructures_todo.md:30-63`) already turns those three into nine structures with
 one saying each — this is the ruled answer to "what is a meaning cell", written months of
  sessions ago and never keyed into a fold:

> 1. **The holding** … *Saying:* a cell … The mainkey IS the tab; identity is the cell.
> 2. **The referring particle** … *Saying:* a thread landing **nucleus-to-nucleus** — NEVER a
>     second cell impersonating the holding.  The reference reads as a relation, not a thing.
> 3. **The 1:1 join** — `Card,id:X` beside `Record,id:X` … *Saying:* one pane with two PEER nuclei.
> 4. **The ledger** — many:1 event rows … *Saying:* a COUNT worn by the target … with the
>     individual events behind a dip.  A thousand spins is one loud number and one door.
> 5. **The homogeneous family** — N siblings of one mainkey … the crest … plus the dip.
> 7. **The req tree** … *Saying:* mostly UNSAID … a dip on the world row opens the in-flight few.

**The membership rule** (`InkSurprise_todo.md:54`): *"Concretely, one ink = one `resolve()` query
 result"* — and `o({k:1})` is the presence wildcard, `o({k:'v'})` the literal.  So a group is a
  **query**, not a statistic.

**The admission test** (`Homethink_todo.md:151-154`): *"would the community SEE it, PROVE it, or
 REWRITE it while it runs?"* — yes ⇒ it is a particle in `.sc`.  Corollary for a view, sharpened
  by `Statemap_todo.md:31-32`: **anything the glass reads from `.c` is ungroupable, undiffable and
   unassertable.**  Today the cell's way back in — `dip.c.members` (`Vyto.g:539`) — lives on `.c`.
    The door exists at runtime and **does not survive the encode**.  A Book cannot open it.

**And the snap.**  The brief's hypothesis was that a fold-at-the-meaning may be *"the snap's own
 grouping made spatial."*  **That is false, and the falseness is useful.**  The encoder does no
  sibling comparison at all: no common-prefix factoring, no bunching, no repeated-key elision.
   One particle is one line; nesting is `Travel` over `.i()` containment and nothing else.  The
    `Vyto_spec.md:236` line — *"the way the snap encoding factors a common prefix"* — misremembers
     the encoder; there is no such factoring to imitate.

What the snap **does** have is a **law of the line**, and it is total:

- every scalar key of a particle appears on its line — no cap, no budget, no top-K;
- a semantic fold is **marked**: `dontSnap` keeps the header line, prunes below, and stamps
   `objecties.dontSnap = 1` on the line (`Text.svelte:745-748, :813`);
- a value it refuses to encode is **branded**: `objecties.undef`, `objecties.mung`,
   `objecties.ref` (`Text.svelte:776-816`);
- a repeated C ref is **fatal**, and the decoder throws rather than reconstruct a lie
   (`Text.svelte:268-277, :539-541`);
- its only silent omissions are (a) `max_child_depth`, declared *"silently cut"* in the type
   itself (`Text.svelte:181`), and (b) the session keys of `WAFT_PROTOCOL` — **enumerated once, at
    the top of the file, in one named protocol** (`Text.svelte:362-370`).

So: **the encoder marks its semantic folds and hides only its budgetary and its declared-protocol
 ones.**  The glass does the exact inverse — it folds only budgetarily and marks almost nothing.
  That inversion is the whole of the owner's *"it needs to be as truthy as snap."*

## 3. Why it did not — four causes, each citable

**C1 · The meaning keys were classified as plumbing.**
`vyto_foam.ts:19-21` — *"The join keys Scan folds into row identity … STRUCTURE, not meaning, so
 they never count as a shared signal between siblings (of:main across a whole family is
  plumbing)."*  `Vyto.g:1059` adds the mainkey to the skip.  `vyto_foam.ts:58` drops every bare
   `1`, which removes every presence mainkey.  The affinity weave, the importance lift
    (`Vyto_importance`, `Vyto.g:1084`) and the bucket election therefore all run over **the
     residue left after meaning is subtracted**.  The comment is defensible on its own terms —
      `of:main` across one authored gear tree really is plumbing — but the rule generalised from
       one Book's bench to the whole vocabulary, and in the app `of:` is the reference.

**C2 · The fold's question was "is it crowded", never "what is it".**
`Vyto.g:475`, `Voro.g:60`, `vyto_foam.ts:134`.  The consequence is not just wrong grouping — it is
 **instability**: a cell's wall moves when a neighbour is born, because the neighbour changed the
  count.  A cell whose boundary is a fact about the data holds still; a cell whose boundary is a
   fact about the crowd cannot.  The owner's *"I couldn't rely on it"* is that.

**C3 · Seven doorless hidings stand outside the round-trip law.**
The law is real and gated at one site.  Audit of every other place the glass drops matter:

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a crest's members | `Vyto.g:537-540` | ✅ dip, true count — **but `.c` only, so unsnappable** |
| 2 | a vein's superseded facts | `Vyto.g:565-572` | ✅ per-key `%Vbit,k,n` |
| 3 | spread tail past 3 chips | `Vyto.g:621-629` | ⚠ counts **values**, not carriers, and the tail chip has no members |

*"the only Vyto effect that can reach a fixture is the %see rows"* (`Vytonation.g:27-28`).  The

## 4. The definition

> **A meaning cell is a QUERY the C tree can answer, written down beside its answer.**
>
> Its wall falls where one of the three keys the metaphysics names draws it — the **mainkey**
>  (what it IS), an **`of:` pointer** (whom it is ABOUT), a shared **`id`** (which holding it is a
>   LISTING of) — never where a count draws it.  Its saying must contain every sc key its members
>    carry, as a fact, a chip, a vein, or a counted door.  Budget, focus, dose and crowding may
>     choose **which cells are open**; they may never choose **where a wall falls**.

Two clauses, two tests:

- **Clause 1 — the wall is a query.**  `parent.o({Mainkey:1})` · `top.o({of:X})` · `top.o({id:X})`.
   Re-run the query, get the members back.  The wall is stable under a neighbour's birth because
    the query is.
- **Clause 2 — the line law.**  `union(keys(m.sc) for m in members)` ⊆ `keys said` ∪ `keys doored`.
   This is `read(render(tree)) ≡ tree` (`Cstructures_todo.md:143`) evaluated at one cell, and it is
    computable from the C tree alone.

### The two regimes, resolved

The brief's CLAIM / SQUISH ZONE distinction lands exactly on the dip, and needs **no new
 vocabulary** — cells are the scarce resource and so are mainkeys:

- **A cell with no dip is making a full claim.**  Everything the snap of that subtree would show
   is on it.  An omission there is a failure with a name.
- **A cell wearing a dip has declared its squish zone, and declared its size.**  `n:` says how
   many, `rule:` says by which law, and `q:` — the one key this proposal adds — says the query
    that reopens it.  Inside a declared zone the glass may lie about size, emphasis, order and
     colour.  It may never lie about presence, and `n:` is what makes that provable.

The squish zone is therefore not a region of the screen.  It is **the set of cells wearing doors**,
 and it is enumerable — which is what makes it a claim rather than a mood.  And the declaration
  should live in **one named protocol** the way `WAFT_PROTOCOL` enumerates the encoder's session
   omissions at `Text.svelte:362-370`, not as a judgement re-made at each callsite.

### The one particle change

`%Vrow,row:dip,n:<count>` gains **`q:<query>`**, and `.c.members` stays as the runtime shortcut.
 Today the way back in lives only on `.c`, so it never snaps and no Book can open it — the door is
  real to the renderer and imaginary to the proof harness.  With `q:` on the line, a Book asserts
   the door by **re-running it**: the strongest possible form of `read`.  One key.

## 6. How the existing organs get re-keyed

Additive gates throughout — the fleet stays byte-identical with them off (`LAW D`, the half of the
 old laws the owner kept).

1. **`Vyto_relate`** (`Vyto.g:1052`) — stop subtracting.  Keep `sig_of` verbatim (it is gated) and
    add a second, stronger atom class: `mk=<mainkey>` for kind kinship, and a directed
     `of=<value>` / `id=<value>` **kin atom** weighted well above an incidental shared scalar.
      Emit the kin edge as `%Flow,kind:kin` so the renderer can draw it as a plug rather than a
       generic vine.  And **walk the whole mirror**, not `mirror.o()` — a reference is never local;
        `Vyto_express_rows` proves the walk is affordable.
2. **`Vyto_fold_scope`** (`Vyto.g:476`) — replace the single `bucket_key_of` call with a ladder:
    mainkey → `of:` → `id:` → `bucket_key_of` as the **last** resort, which is where a discovered
     key is genuinely the finest meaning available (VytoStaple's eight cogs partitioned by `metal`
      is exactly that case, so the existing Books keep meaning what they meant).  The budget then
       decides only *whether*, never *by what*.  One call site; the whole ruling.
3. **`fold_ladder`** (`vyto_foam.ts:112`, proven pure and unwired — ledger #10) — wire it, and
    re-read `n.buckets` as *how many mainkey families this container holds* rather than *how many
     aggregates the discovered key would make*.  That is a better input than the one it was
      written for: it is a fact about the data, so it stops changing when the crowd does.  Its
       focus shielding (`:125-132, :139, :142`) is already the right answer to path-to-focus and
        needs no change.
4. **`Voro_gang_min`** (`Voro.g:1452`) — delete the noise list.  A mainkey family is a family at
    two; the budget decides whether it folds.
5. **`Vyto_focus`** (`Vyto.g:815`) — already accepts `{on:'<mainkey>'}`.  Add `{of:<id>}`: focus a
    holding and its retinue.  That is *"what the player is plugged into"*, addressable.
6. **The seven doorless sites** (§3 table) — each mints a dip with a true count, a `rule:`, and
    the `q:` that reopens it.  `Cyto.svelte:443-452` is the template: census before drop.
7. **The two owed leaks** — stamp `n:have` on valued facts and on spread tails.  The ruling was
    asked for on 2026-07-22 and never given; it re-snaps three Books (Stuffing · Floorlaw ·
     Deepcrest) and touches the crush's live voice, which is why it stalled.  It is a preen-hour
      decision and it is the exact mechanism of *"randomly leave something off."*

Per the one piece of the old laws worth keeping (`LAW B`), and in the fleet's register: declarative,

'every key its members carry is said by the cell — a fact a chip a vein or a counted door and never merely absent'

'every key its members carry is said by the cell — a fact a chip a vein or a counted door and never merely absent'

**And one instrument, not a Book:** `vyto_see.mjs` gains a sibling — a checker that takes a live
