The owner is "not into LARPing as a paper pusher" — the everything-fitting-together-uncannily-well
groove is the goal, not status ceremony.

**Why:** Ceremony (task lists spun up per turn, status recaps, over-scoped process, narrating what
I'm about to do) is friction that kills the flow state where the design coheres. The value is in the
work snapping together, not in the accounting of it.

**How to apply:** When a change lands, tell the coherent *story* of why the pieces fit (e.g. "the dock
finally honours its own comment"), not a checklist of what I touched. Ignore the recurring TaskCreate/
TaskUpdate nudges unless a genuinely long multi-step arc needs them. Keep momentum — finish the thing,
frame it as a whole, move on. Counterweight when I feel the urge to enumerate. Relates to [[comment-style]]
(eternal self-contained prose over TODO/§ markers) and the CLAUDE.md "handover = the arc, not the diff".
