**Owner direction, 2026-07-04.** Most Musu* Story Books were built to prove ONE aspect of an algorithm as
 it was written (a rate curve, a fill mode, a spin, a cursor advance). That isolation earned its keep DURING
  CONSTRUCTION, but the mature state is a small set of tests that exercise the whole algorithm in ONE
   real-context place, with the trivial single-aspect proofs folded in and RETIRED. "They prove aspects of an
    algorithm that we just want brought into one place." The aim is **fewer, realer Books — not a museum of
     micro-proofs.**

**The scaffolding tell:** a Book's witnesses are soft (something EXISTS / time PASSED / N happened) or its
 one hard/differential claim is already made by a real integration Book.

**First to retire: MusuRadio.** Its only differential claim `helps` (Glide beats no-control) duplicates
 MusuGlide (and on real music, MusuCrate); `ready`/`sustained`/`many_tracks` are soft; its unique bit
  (real-time AUDIBLE playback) is unwitnessable on a muted gesture-gated runner. A demo in a test's clothing.
   Fold its intent into **MusuReco's audio-proof** (B PLAYS its byte-exact real replica through the unlocked
    voice — the deferred §9.10 "demo that IS the app"): one honest Book = real supply chain AND real playback.

**Keepers (for now):** the rate-control/slice family — Signal/Glide/Tune/Edge/Stock/Stream/Conceal — each
 isolate one mechanism on a CONTROLLED signal where determinism is a FEATURE (Conceal's seam measurement,
  Glide's Schmitt band; real music would confound them). Keep until a real-audio integration Book exercises
   the same mechanisms end-to-end on real music over the real transport, THEN re-audit each: does it still
    prove something the integration Book doesn't? If not, retire it too.

**Real-music placement (settled):** real testsounds/ only at MusuCrate (decode) + MusuReco (transcode +
 supply chain + the coming audio-proof); everything below stays synth on purpose. TODO lives in Radio_spec §6
  "Consolidation". See [[repli-protocol]] for the audio-proof vessel, [[story-books-catalog]] for the family.
