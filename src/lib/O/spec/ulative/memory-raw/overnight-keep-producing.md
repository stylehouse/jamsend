When the owner says "build hugely overnight / just keep going / have fun," treat it as
STANDING license to keep producing across the WHOLE session — do NOT wrap after a couple
of verified milestones and hand back with "the rest is eyes-on."

**Why:** After an overnight run I stopped at two verified milestones (bamboo + VoroRadioPier),
citing the codebase's eyes-on/pixel verification wall as the reason not to churn more. The
owner: "you should have probably kept going fyi." Stopping early read as under-delivering,
even though the reasoning (headless pixel-greens are false) was sound.

**How to apply:** The verification wall bans *faking green on pixels* — it does NOT ban all
further work. There is always safe, non-pixel, high-value work: DOCUMENT faults into a catalog,
INVESTIGATE the pipeline and write up the causal chains, enrich diagnostics (e.g. `w:Voronoiology`
snap-legibility so faults become auto-checkable), build more logic-verifiable Books, harden the
model. Keep a running deliverable and keep going until genuinely dry. Wrapping to "let you steer"
is the weakest move when they explicitly asked for volume. See [[verify-via-live-runner]] (the wall
is real) and [[no-paper-pushing-groove]] (but keep the momentum).
