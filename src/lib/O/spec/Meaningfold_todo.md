**The render is a snap** — same truth standard, different medium. A snap that silently drops a
 particle is a bug; a render that silently drops one is the same bug, and the glass never said so,
  so every omission got recorded as a "choice" or an "owed station" instead of a red.

> **A meaning cell is a QUERY the C tree can answer, written down beside its answer.**
>
> Its wall falls where one of the three keys the metaphysics names draws it — the **mainkey** (what
>  it IS), an **`of:`** (whom it is ABOUT), a shared **`id`** (which holding it LISTS) — never where
>   a count draws it. Its saying must contain every sc key its members carry, as a fact, a chip, a
>    vein, or a counted door. Budget, focus, dose and crowding may choose **which cells are open**;
>     they may never choose **where a wall falls**.

**The two regimes need no new vocabulary; they land on the dip.** A cell with **no dip** makes a
 full claim — everything the snap of that subtree would show is on it, and an omission is a failure
  with a name. A cell **wearing a dip** has declared its squish zone *and its size*: `n:` how many,
   `rule:` by which law, `q:` the query that reopens it. Inside a declared zone the glass may lie
    about size, emphasis, order and colour. It may never lie about presence, and `n:` is what makes
     that provable. So the squish zone is not a region of the screen — it is **the set of cells
      wearing doors**, and it is enumerable. The declaration wants ONE named protocol, the way
       `WAFT_PROTOCOL` enumerates the encoder's omissions (`Text.svelte:362-370`), never a judgement
        re-made per callsite.

## 2. Why it did not fold at the meaning — the two causes

- **The fold subtracted meaning before it looked.** `SIG_JOINS` strikes the join keys as
   "plumbing"; `Vyto_relate` strikes the mainkey; `bucket_key_of` skips every bare `1` presence
    marker. Every fold therefore partitioned **the residue left after meaning was removed**, and
     entered by a count. The owner's *"hard to get it to fold at the meaning"* is exactly this: the
      meaning was not in the input.
- **The round-trip law is gated at one site out of eleven.** `read(render(tree)) ≡ tree, modulo
   declared dips` (`Cstructures_todo.md:143`). Eleven hiding sites audited, **seven doorless** —
    including the two measured on 2026-07-22 with a human ruling owed. That is *"randomly leave
     something off"*, verbatim.

**A correction worth carrying.** Folding-at-the-meaning is NOT "the snap's grouping made spatial" —
 the encoder does no sibling factoring at all, and `Vyto_spec.md:236` misremembers it. What the snap
  has is a **law of the line**: it MARKS its semantic folds (`objecties.dontSnap`) and hides only
   budgetary and declared-protocol ones. The glass does the exact inverse. Fixing the inversion is
    the work.
