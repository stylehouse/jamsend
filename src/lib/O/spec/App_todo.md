# App_todo — the ramble over the app model: a person, their bodies, their friends, and the music between them

**What this doc is.** A map over the model docs, not a second design. jamsend's *app* — the thing a person
 meets, as opposed to the machine it runs on (`Coding_guide.md`, `Cluster_spec.md`) — is modelled across
  `_todo`s that each own one organ: `Crew_todo.md` (souls and bodies), `Love_todo.md` + `Radio_circuit_todo.md`
   (the ♥ and the heard Mag), `SoundPooling_todo.md` (the pool), `Reach_todo.md` (cross-body work),
    `Persistence_todo.md` (what survives a reload), `Onboarding_todo.md` / `Presence_todo.md` (the Door and who
     is here). This doc walks them in the order a person's life meets them, names the ghost verb and the Book
      that gates each mechanism, and quotes the owner's rulings. **The per-area docs stay authoritative**;
       where two disagree, the one whose own header claims precedence wins, said in place, not resolved here.
        The front door to the code is `wormhole/Everything/toc.snap` (generated by
         `scripts/wafts_everything.mjs`): one `What` per area — `Ghost/Swarm/Easy`, `Ghost/Music/Cave`,
          `Ghost/Music/Ality`, `Ghost/Net/Easy` — listing the main verbs as `Point,method:X` and the fixtured
           Books under `Funkcion:CreduFunk`.

The posture is `Homethink_todo.md §1`: *"turn every kind of state — a song, a test, a friendship, an error —
 into the same legible living matter, held where a group can see it, prove it, and rewrite it while it runs.
  Then pour music through it first."* Every mechanism below is a particle, snapped, and sworn by a Book on a
   live runner.

## 1. The person and their bodies

**A person is a soul: one signing key.** `%Identity,prepub:<eed…>` owns its keys as particles —
 `/Crew,soul:<pub>` › `Key,pub,secret` › `mate:<prepub>,role,pub` — from birth (*every identity is born a
  crew of one*, `Crew_todo.md §3`; accessors in `src/lib/O/Funk/Crewkeys.ts`, verbs `Swarm_keys`,
   `Swarm_soul`, `Swarm_myrow`, `Swarm_signas` in `Ghost/S/Swarm.g`). Nothing about a key lives in `.c`
    (*"everything is snappable… a secret's protection is WHERE IT IS ALLOWED TO GO, not where it is stored"*,
     `Crew_todo.md §9`); Story mungs `secret`, the `page` export protocol strips `Key` + `Crew`.

**A body is (store × soul)** — one device, its own keypair, its prepub its address; a vessel is one tab.
 **Captain and Cave are crew ROLES, read off `mate%role`**: the Captain is *the mate whose row says Captain
  — it hellos and signs AS THE SOUL*; a Cave *hellos and signs as its own pub, proves crew by its
   `Grant:Crew`* (`Crew_todo.md §4a`). Every crewmate carries the whole `/Crew` tree, soul secret included
    (*"super low security"*), so **"make X the Captain" = flip `role` on two rows, no key moves** —
     `Swarm_crew_captain(w, ident, prepub)`; a founder standing down is `Swarm_crew_standdown`. The ledger IS
      the mutex: *"the last soul-signed ledger a mate hears REPLACES its own. No epoch, no era compare, no wall
       clock."* Gate: **SwarmHelm** (`Ghost/Story/SwarmTesting.g`). ⚠ Roles are NOT folder-vs-no-folder: a
        body with a music folder wears `%Organ,kind:trove` on its `%Body` roster row (`Swarm_organ_of`),
         whichever role it holds (a slip conflating the two was made and corrected in `SoundPooling_todo.md
          §0.0`, 2026-09-20; `Crew_todo.md` wins by its own header, *"what stands here wins"*). *"A phone with no
           folder is the usual Captain"* (`Love_todo.md`). Roles *"are NOT a trust hierarchy… they are all YOU"*
            (`§1`).

**LinkDevice is the ceremony** that grows a second body: URL = `?Iz=<token>#fc=<secret>`, the secret in the
 fragment never the relay; the walk is ONE legible req (`req:Ferry_soul` / `req:Ferry_cave`, one write
  chokepoint `Swarm_ferry_phase`, readers derive from `Swarm_ferry_facts`); the ferry carries the account
   AND `/Crew` fc-sealed; the merge runs `Swarm_crew_join`; the Cave's ask is a `%Reach,for:ferry`
    (`Swarm_ferry_ask` → `Swarm_ferry_verdict`, W2). Face: `src/lib/O/ui/LinkDevice.svelte` (one cell, one
     phase at a time). Gates: **InvWalk 8/8** (grant lands, Cave keeps own key), **InvFerry**, **InvSeal**,
      **SwarmInvite** (spent-QR tooth), **SwarmSpread**, `crew-cert-test.ts`.

**The no-folder life is a complete life.** *"The folder is an UPGRADE, not a gate. Listen-only (Dexie
 identity + OPFS pool) is a COMPLETE life — a phone's life"* (`Onboarding_todo.md §3`). The splash's one
  OPEN SHARE bar (`boot_gate.svelte.ts`) harvests AudioContext + folder in one click; *"🎧 listen without a
   folder"* is the refuse. A phone therefore has no `.jamsend/` and **the House stash is its only home** (§7).

## 2. Friends and crew

**A friendship is a pair of signed grants on a `%Pier`** under my `%Peering` (*"Peering = who we listen as,
 Pier = who we dial"*, `Crew_todo.md §9`): `Grant:Music,by,for` both ways, ended only by a `%NotGrant`
  tombstone (`Swarm_pier_forget`, region `revocation`). The invite is an `%Idzeug` — a single-use signed token
   or a printed blotter (`Swarm_token`, `Swarm_iz_claim`; face `InvitePanel.svelte`). **The Door** is first
    contact: `Swarm_station_up` stands the relay socket, `pier_hello/accept/confirm` seal (`Swarm_seal`), a
     refusal is a `%rebuff` that names itself (`Swarm_rebuff`, forgiven when a Pier seals —
      `Swarm_rebuff_forgive`, narrow by design). Face: `DoorFace.svelte`. Gates: **SwarmStaple** (handshake
       + forged-presig teeth; beat 7 byte-inert canary), **SwarmDoor**, **SwarmSteal**.

**Crew is grant-gated membership; the roster is a routing cache.** *"When the question is 'is this one of
 mine?', /Crew answers. The roster is a cache and may be missing rows; it may never be the authority"*
  (`Crew_todo.md §0.0`). The crew seal mints `Grant:Music` both ways too, so `Swarm_share_granted` admits a
   crewmate with no special case. A friend trusts a Cave AS the soul through `Swarm_voucher_ok`'s cert-crew
    road (voucher `{from: body prepub, grant: <Grant:Crew>}`, plus *no `NotGrant:Crew` for that pub*). An
     ejection **travels** (`Swarm_crew_eject` → the `crew` frame → `Swarm_crew_heard`), and the pier's cached
      voucher is cleared on hearing it (memory `voucher-cache-outlives-revocation`). The ✕ on the Door means
       eject (Captain on a mate), resign (Cave on Captain, `Swarm_crew_leave`) or unbond.

**Presence is asked, never guessed, and only ever SUBTRACTS.** `Ghost/N/Presence.g` (`Presence_ask`,
 `Presence_live` three-valued — `null` is DON'T KNOW — `Presence_worth_sending`); *"is this soul online"* =
  the union of its crewmates' presences. Gated by `scripts/Presence.spec.ts` + `relay-test.ts`, NOT a Book
   (runners hold 0 piers — `Presence_todo.md §0`). The relay (`src/lib/server/relay.ts`, `Cluster_spec.md`)
    only routes; a tab's ping ack carries a real role: `editor` · `runner` · `player`.

## 3. What a body holds

Four homes, and *"mixing any two of them is the mistake"* (`Radio_circuit_todo.md §0.5`):

| home | shape | continuity |
|---|---|---|
| **Mine** — my collection | `Mine,pub:<me> > stock,pub > Mag:shuffle,pub > Cloud,page:N > Record,id` — my disk read as a Mag (`Ra_home_self`, `Ra_mag_page`) | forever; the disk is the truth |
| **Theirs** — a friend's mirror | `Theirs,pub:<them> > stock > Mag:shuffle,pub:<them>` — heads + preview chunks, arrived as ONE husk fragment (`Ra_offer_stock` → `Repli_merge`) | none; session matter, never berths |
| **SoundPile** — the pool | `SoundPile,pub:<me> > stock > Record,id:<lofi>,of:<orig>,grade:ogg128,path:pool/…` in OPFS | policy in, budget out |
| **`Mag:heard,pub:<me>`** | under `%Identity` — references into the edge | durable; the 8th pillar |

**Repli is how the edge is walked.** `Ghost/N/Repli.g`: *"a landscape of ONE type (C) with clearly defined
 frontiers | paginations… You WALK the landscape, you don't GET it."* A `%Record` appearing on a shelf is
  noticed by that shelf's Seem (`Repli_sent_se`) and the notice IS the offer; the far side upserts by
   `Repli_identity_keys` (`Mag:[pub] Cloud:[page] Record:[id] Card:[id,pub]`); `Ra_pull_beat` pulls pages with
    `repli_want`; `Ra_stage` stamps a mirrored Record's fill (`husk|pulling|landing|previewed|parked|whole`).
     Bytes are chunk particles — `%Preview,seq` then `%Stream,seq`, one seq space (`Ra.g` header). Gates:
      **RepliUpsert · MusuReplica** (`MusuTesting.g`), **MusuRaStream · MusuRaChase** (`RaTesting.g`),
       **SwarmShare** (the standing session, `Swarm_share_beat`). **A body serves only its own Mine**
        (`Ra_home_self` is the only registered caster); the pile and the mirrors are never re-offered.

## 4. Hearing and reacting

**The radio dials; the heard Mag remembers.** `Radio_dial` ranks the rungs (join in flight → `Mag:Lineup` →
 Streams peek → pool → own shuffle); `Radio_lineup_fill` keeps 20 Cards ahead round-robining Mine, each
  Theirs and the pool, skipping ids already heard (`Ghost/M/Radio.g`; face `RadioFace.svelte`). A track
   opening is `Heard_mark`; a play-through with a person present (`humdinger`) is `Heard_through` → `mire+1`,
    **no bump** (*a play-through is not worth a disk write*, `Heard.g` header).

**One Card per `(id, pub)`, find-or-create, never re-minted** — `Mag:heard,pub:<me> > Cloud,page:N,created_at
 > Card,id:<track>,pub:<holder>`. Two stages: *heard* is OBLIQUE (`id, pub, mire` — bare ids, Mag_todo §6b's
  ruling); *taken* clones the listing (`title, artist, dir, path, bytes, body_hash`, then `keep:<their
   keep-id>`) at the consent moment. Every road and verdict is a scalar on the Card and nothing else:
    `take · at · nay · meh · via:<body> · handed:<name> · into:pool · keep · held · unvouched · landfail,why ·
     unseen` (`Heard.g` THE HANDOFF header). Rules ride the Mag line: `heard_ttl=30, take_ttl=90` (`Heard_gc`
      drops an unwanted hearing; a heart is *never* dropped by a clock — `Heard_gave_up` is a WORD, `Heard_word`).

**Reactions, not toggles** (`Love_todo.md §0`, owner 2026-09-15): **Yay** (♥ = `Heard_take`, via
 `Radio_like`), **Nay** (`Heard_nay` / `Radio_nay` — evicts from the pool, bars future draws, *never deletes
  a heisted file*), **Meh** (`Heard_meh`, written by the radio on a skip under `Radio_meh_ms`=20 s).
   ***"There is no unlove, anywhere"*** — a second press re-affirms; ✕ on a Haul row retires a KEEP.
    `Heard_barred_ids` is what the pool never draws. **The taste tally** is `Heard_tally`: *take 3 · keep 2 ·
     mire 1 · a bare hearing 0*. A heart on my own music (`pub === me`) is a taste fact, nothing owed. A
      pooled ♥ names the ORIGINAL with no holder (`Heard_take_id/_pub`; `Heard_holder_of` resolves off the
       `%Theirs` mirrors at haul time, crew first). Gate: **MusuHeard** (`HeistTesting.g`; owner re-swears the
        toggle step). ⚠ `Love_todo.md §2` still says *"a second ♥ removes it (Heard_unwant)"* — stale within
         its own doc; its §0 wins (`Heard_unwant` is deleted). `Radio_circuit_todo.md §0/§9.8` describe the
          toggle as history, by their own banner.
